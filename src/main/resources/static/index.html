Each candle runs from the first reading (open) to the last (close) in its period; the wick spans the lowest and highest charge. Day view shows hourly candles; week and year show one candle per day. Readings are taken every 5 minutes.

Energy sent to the grid (split into what came straight from solar and what the battery discharged) and energy bought from the grid (split into what went straight to the house and what charged the battery). Both charts share one scale so they can be compared. Money is at today's tariff for the whole history; import is time-of-use, so it's split by when it was imported. Each day is fetched from GivEnergy just after midnight; today isn't included until then.
