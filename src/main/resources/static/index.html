When energy moved

The last two days, half-hourly, including today. Shaded bands are the off-peak window. Bars are import; the line is export.

Energy sent to the grid (split into what came straight from solar and what the battery discharged) and energy bought from the grid (split into what went straight to the house and what charged the battery). Both charts share one scale so they can be compared. Each day keeps the prices from when it was first saved, so a tariff change leaves older months alone. The net adds one standing charge per day and subtracts export earnings. Daily bars run through yesterday; today is in the side panel and the half-hourly chart.
